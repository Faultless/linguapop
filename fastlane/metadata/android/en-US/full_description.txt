LinguaPop is a reader for people learning Japanese. It pulls the day's news
from Japanese outlets and lays it out as a newspaper you can actually read at
your level — every word colour-coded by JLPT difficulty, every word tappable
for a dictionary entry.

<b>The newsstand</b>

Papers are fetched whole rather than article by article: pull today's edition,
or the latest ten stories, from NHK News Web Easy, NHK News Web and the
Mainichi Shimbun. The front page sets them like a Japanese daily — masthead,
dateline, two columns of headlines with the images the source published.
Swipe left or right to change paper.

<b>Reading</b>

Tap any word for its dictionary entry, readings, and the grammar of the form
it's in: LinguaPop analyses conjugation chains, so 食べていました is one word
with its own explanation rather than five loose morphemes. Save words to a
vocabulary list and export it to AnkiDroid as a TSV.

Words are coloured by JLPT level — N5 through N1 — using the bundled JLPT
vocabulary lists. Words outside those lists are estimated from their kanji and
marked with a dashed underline, so nothing on a real news page is left grey.

<b>Your own books too</b>

Import EPUB or plain-text files, optionally paired with a translation, and
read them with the same tools. Original, translated, or side-by-side. Eleven
reading themes plus five seasonal ones, adjustable type, scrolling or paged
layout.

<b>Offline and private</b>

Japanese analysis runs entirely on the device with a bundled MeCab tokeniser
and dictionary. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no data leaves the
device except the requests that fetch the articles and dictionary entries you
ask for.
